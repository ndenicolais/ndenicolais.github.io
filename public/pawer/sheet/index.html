Pawer

Every paw deserves
a better future

The app to report stray or distressed dogs and cats, help get them rescued and find lost pets.

Android Web

Pawer is a gamble

Lots of people see an animal on the street, but often nobody knows who to tell. Pawer wants to change that: whoever meets it reports it, whoever sees it again updates it, and whoever can help knows where to go.

It only works if lots of us use it. Pawer is being tested: try it and tell us what to improve.

What it does

Everything you need to help them

Report an animal

Photos, colours, condition and location: a few steps are enough to let people know where it is.

Lost pets

Lost your pet? Post an announcement: people in the area get a notification and can call you.

Map

The open reports in your region and directions to the spot.

Sightings

If you see an animal again, mark its new position: the whole community keeps its history up to date.

Shelters

Shelters and useful numbers in your region, to call straight from the app.

Notifications

New reports and lost pets in your area, and sightings of the ones you follow.

How it works

Three steps, one rescue

You see it

You come across a stray, injured or lost dog or cat.

You report it

Take a photo, describe how it is doing and the location fills itself in.

We follow it

The report appears on the map and every new sighting updates its position, until someone rescues it.

Installation

How to install it

On Android

Scan and download

ndenicolais.github.io/pawer/Pawer.apk Download the APK · v1.1.0
  1. Download the APK to your phone and open it.
  2. If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you are opening it from (browser, WhatsApp, Files…).
  3. Tap Install, then open Pawer.
  4. Sign up with your email or with Google and choose your region.

During installation Android may warn you that the app is unsafe or show "App blocked to protect your device": it does this for every app installed outside the Play Store, where Pawer is not available yet. Tap More details → Install anyway. Pawer only uses the data described in the privacy policy. Camera, location and notifications are requested only when needed.

On iPhone

In testing

  1. Open pawerapp.web.app in Safari, or scan the QR code at the bottom.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Pawer from its icon like any other app.

It also works in your computer's browser, at the same address.

Why it exists

In memory of Orsetto

Pawer was born for Orsetto, a little brown and white furball found on the street. He was kindness and innocence in dog form, endlessly loyal from the very first moment. Even if only for a short time, he made his whole family's life better.

This app is a way to give back everything he gave: helping other dogs and cats like him find someone to take care of them.

Try it and spread the word

Every report is a step towards a rescue and, maybe, a new home.
