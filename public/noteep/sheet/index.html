Noteep is reborn

Noteep 2.0 has been rewritten from scratch: notes, tasks and calendar now live together and sync to your account. I want to find out whether it really makes your day simpler.

Use it for a few days instead of your notepad or sticky notes: try it and tell me what to improve.

What it does

Your whole day in one app

Notes

Formatted text, checklists and voice notes, each with its own font and colours, to share with other Noteep users.

Tasks and calendar

Tasks grouped by day and a calendar with birthdays, name days and event locations.

Notifications

Timely reminders for notes, tasks and events, plus widgets for your Home screen.

Sync and backup

Everything on your account, offline too, with backups and PDF export.

How it works

From idea to reminder in three steps

Jot it down

Write a note or a list, or record a voice note.

Plan it

Turn it into a task with a due date or a calendar event.

Get reminded

Pick a day and time and Noteep will remind you at the right moment.

Installation

How to install it

On Android

Scan and download

github.com/ndenicolais/Noteep/releases/latest Download the APK · v2.0.0
  1. Open the link, scroll down to Assets and tap the .apk file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you are opening it from (browser, WhatsApp, Files…).
  3. Tap Install, then open Noteep.
  4. Sign in with Google or sign up with your email.

During installation Android may warn you that the app is unsafe or show "App blocked to protect your device": it does this for every app installed outside the Play Store, where Noteep is not available yet. Tap More details → Install anyway. Noteep only uses the data described in the privacy policy. Microphone and notification access are requested only when needed.

Already have Noteep 1.x? Uninstall it before installing 2.0: your notes, tasks and events come back as soon as you sign in, while audio notes and automatic backups stay in the old app only. Requires Android 7.0 or later, 64-bit. For now Noteep is available on Android only.
